Our Story

Fresh ingredients. Distinctive flavors.
A warm welcome.

Asia Kitchen is a family affair: Chef Sally in the kitchen, Pat at the door. Every plate starts with fresh ingredients and the bold, balanced flavors of Thailand: sour, sweet, salty and as spicy as you dare.

S

Sally

Chef

Sally created the recipes that made Asia Kitchen. Her food is known for being crisp and fresh, with distinctive, boldly blended flavors. Start with her Phad Thai or a Thai salad.

P

Pat

Host & Manager

Pat is the warm welcome you get at the door, always happy to stop by your table to make sure everything is just right.

✦

Made your way

Mild · Medium · Hot

Tell us your spice level and we'll cook to it. Many dishes can be prepared gluten-free, and almost any entrée can be made vegetarian.
