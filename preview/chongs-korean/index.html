Our story

Find the complexity in our simplicity.

Chong's is a small, family restaurant on the west side of San Antonio serving simple, traditional Korean food. For years Chong dreamed of opening a place where she could cook for everyone. This is that dream.

The recipes are the ones she learned from her mother and grandmother. First time here? Start with a meat dish, and get the Ya Ki Man Du to share.
