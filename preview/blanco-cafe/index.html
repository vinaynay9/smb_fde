Our Story

Homemade recipes & hard work since 1974

Alejandro F. San Miguel opened the first Blanco Cafe at 1720 Blanco Rd. to provide for his wife and their 10 children. His goal was simple: a great meal at a price anyone could afford.

“Their breakfast is our lunch or dinner.”

That's how Alejandro explained serving breakfast all day, and it's still our trademark. Today the San Miguel family, led by Albert San Miguel, keeps those recipes going in two locations.
