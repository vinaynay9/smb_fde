Our story

From a father's recipes to four generations.

Manuel Bedoy learned the art of baking sweet bread from his father in Mexico. In 1961, he and his wife Emma opened their own bakery, and with perseverance and hard work it grew into one of the most recognized bakeries in San Antonio.

Today the Bedoy family keeps those traditions alive, one tray of conchas at a time.

Traditions

Breads San Antonio celebrates with.

Pan de Muerto

The family first baked it at the request of the Rector of San Fernando Cathedral. Today it is part of San Antonio's Día de los Muertos celebration.
