Our Story

From Canton to West Commerce

Four generations of the Lim family, one family recipe book, and a lot of fish with gravy.

Early 1900s

Papa Lim's journey

Koon Yen "Papa" Lim leaves Canton, China, at age 13. After time in California, he settles in San Antonio with a dream of his own.

1932

Golden Star opens

With his savings, Papa Lim opens Golden Star Cafe downtown. It becomes known for scratch-made pies, servers carrying full orders up each arm, and a kitchen that never stopped cooking.

1960s–70s

Moving along Commerce

The cafe moves twice along West Commerce Street. Papa Lim and his wife, Nu Lee Wong Lim, run it with their children by their side.

1983

821 W. Commerce

Golden Star opens at its current home on the corner of South Frio, the gateway to the West Side, with a ribbon cutting attended by then-Mayor Henry Cisneros.

2022

90 years strong

The City of San Antonio honors our 90th anniversary with a proclamation, and Golden Star is recognized as a San Antonio Legacy Business. Today Bo, Alex and the Lim family keep Papa Lim's legacy cooking.

Many things have come and gone since 1932, but throughout that time, one entity has become a fixture.Former Mayor Henry Cisneros, at our 90th anniversary
