Our place

A little patio, a lot of heart.

Tucked into a shopping center at Wurzbach and Lockhill-Selma, Pam's has been a North Central San Antonio favorite for years: the place you meet a friend for lunch, bring the family for dinner, or settle in on the patio with a glass of something good.

The menu wanders happily from Texas to Thailand and back, with soups made in-house every day, salads, sandwiches and tortas at lunch, and steaks, seafood, pasta and Thai favorites at dinner. And then there are Pam's cakes.
