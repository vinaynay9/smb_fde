Our Story

Three decades of North Indian hospitality

For more than thirty years, Star of India has been a gathering place on Anderson Lane: for weekday lunch, family birthdays and the Sunday buffet that brings everyone back. Owner Vir Singh and the team cook the dishes of North India with care, and serve them with the warmth of a family table.

Everything is prepared with vegetable oil or butter, and nearly every entrée can be made mild, medium, spicy or extra spicy. Just tell us how you like it.
