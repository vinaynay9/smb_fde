Our story

Same family. Same corner. Since 1962.

What started with a neighborhood bakery on East 9th Street became Joe's Bakery & Coffee Shop when Joe and Paula Avila opened on East 7th. Today the Avila family is still behind the counter, and the regulars still know everyone by name.

  1. 1935La OrientalSophia DeLa'O runs La Oriental Grocery & Bakery on East 9th Street.
  2. 1962Joe's opens on East 7thJoe and Paula Avila open Joe's Bakery & Coffee Shop at 2305 E. 7th St.
  3. 2023James Beard America's ClassicNamed the Texas honoree of the James Beard Foundation's America's Classics award.
  4. TodayFour generationsStill family-run, still making tortillas and pan dulce every morning.
