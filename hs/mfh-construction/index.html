What we do

Services

MFH Construction is an owner-run construction and remodeling company in Nolanville. Malo builds cedar and pine fences, screened patio rooms and paver patios, remodels bathrooms and floors, adds custom cabinetry and doors, and finishes commercial interiors.

Fences & gates

Cedar and pine privacy fences, board-on-board runs and Z-brace gates, plus storm repairs.

Bathroom remodels & tile

Bathrooms redone with tiled tub surrounds, new vanities and design suggestions.

Flooring & interior paint

Wood-look plank floors through living areas and halls, and fresh interior paint.

Patios & screen rooms

Screened patio enclosures, covered patios and paver patio pads.

Cabinetry, doors & custom builds

Custom cabinets and built-in shelving, door installs, and builds like a backyard greenhouse.

Commercial build-outs

Shop and office interiors with new flooring, slatwall and built-in shelving.
