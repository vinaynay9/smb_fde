Questions

Frequently asked

Do you take small concrete jobs?

Yes. Reviewers describe small slabs and pads where Chris came out to do the work himself and treated them like a commercial account.

How fast can Chris come out to quote?

One customer had Chris at the house for an estimate the same day they called.

What happens if it rains before my pour?

The pour waits until conditions are right. One reviewer had a rain delay, then the crew finished quickly and smoothly once it dried out.

Can you fix drainage problems in my yard?

Yes. Semper Fi grades and reworks yards to move water away, then finishes with rock beds or landscaping.

Which areas do you work in?

New Braunfels, Canyon Lake, Seguin, San Marcos, Schertz, Cibolo and Garden Ridge.
