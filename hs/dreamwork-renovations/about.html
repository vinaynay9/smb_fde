About DreamWork

DreamWork Renovations is a husband-and-wife remodeling company in Spring, near The Woodlands. Blaga plans each project and keeps you updated; Sukh, with 20+ years of hands-on experience in London and Texas, leads the craftsmanship.

Our story

Husband-and-wife owned: Blaga and Sukh

DreamWork Renovations is run by a husband-and-wife team. Blaga handles planning and communication, so you always know what is happening next. Sukh leads the work on site, bringing more than 20 years of hands-on remodeling experience from London and Texas.

Their work runs from full kitchen and bath remodels to stacked stone fireplaces, floors, paint and leak repairs. Projects often start with a material board, with cabinet doors, hardware, tile and flooring picked together with the client.
