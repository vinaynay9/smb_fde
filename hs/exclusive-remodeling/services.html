Questions

Frequently asked

Do you have a showroom?

Yes. The showroom is at 26103 I-45, Suite 103A, in Spring (77380). It is open weekdays, and Saturday visits are by appointment.

Can we live at home during the remodel?

Yes. Reviewers describe the crew covering rooms in plastic every day, keeping construction dust contained and cleaning up before they leave.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

It depends on the scope. One reviewer's kitchen took five days from start to finish, and another homeowner had two baths and a downstairs floor done in about two months.

Will I get a detailed estimate?

Yes. One reviewer describes a line-by-line itemized estimate, and others say their job finished within budget and on the time estimate.

Is the work warranted?

Yes. Exclusive Remodeling offers a homeowner's warranty and carries liability, workers' compensation and contract works insurance. Customers say Amine honors it.

Do you build patio covers and outdoor kitchens?

Yes. Amine's team adds covered patios, outdoor living spaces and built-in outdoor kitchens in Spring and The Woodlands.
