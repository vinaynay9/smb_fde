Frequently asked

How quickly can Mario get to my house?

Reviewers often get a same-day or next-day visit. One called at 9 in the morning and had a working A/C by 5:30 that evening.

Do you repair restaurant walk-ins and ice machines?

Yes. Mario services walk-in coolers and freezers, reach-ins, prep tables and ice machines for San Antonio restaurants.

Yes. Several reviewers called Mario after another company missed the problem, and he found it, including a bad breaker.

Monday to Friday 9 AM to 5 PM and Saturday 9 AM to noon. Call to ask about urgent A/C or refrigeration problems.
