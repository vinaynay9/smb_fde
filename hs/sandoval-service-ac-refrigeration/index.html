What we do

Home A/C and commercial refrigeration

Sandoval Service A/C & Refrigeration is Mario Sandoval, a licensed A/C contractor in North Central San Antonio. He handles sales, service and installation on all makes and models, from a family's central A/C to a restaurant's walk-in cooler.

Commercial refrigeration

Second opinions
