## Things worth knowing

- **Others are pitching them too.** Someone has already put up unsolicited sample sites on Cloudflare (`pages.dev`) for #1 Texas Elite and #3 Climate Masters. They still don't have a site of their own, but these two owners have probably heard a website pitch before. That's a reason to make our version clearly better, or to swap them out.
- **Arise Services (#8)** has been in business since about 2002, so it may have had a website that lapsed. That could be a good angle ("your old site is gone").
- **What I skipped:** the Google results for "HVAC repair Austin" are full of fake lead-generation listings with names like "Tundra HVAC Repair Austin" and out-of-state phone numbers. I left out anything that looked like that, anything under about 10 reviews, and anything rated under 4.3.
- **Emails are the hard part.** None of these listings shows an email address on Google. See the note in the thread.
